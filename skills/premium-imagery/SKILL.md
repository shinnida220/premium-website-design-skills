# Premium Imagery

Select imagery that carries meaning and supports the art direction. Images should not be decorative filler.

## Workflow
1. Identify the job of each visual: explain product, demonstrate outcome, establish mood, provide evidence, guide attention, or support navigation.
2. Define subject, context, composition, lighting, palette, aspect ratio, focal point, and crop behavior.
3. Prefer authentic product screenshots, approved photography, commissioned assets, or licensed assets that match the audience and concept.
4. Evaluate relevance, quality, consistency, representation, and rights before selection.
5. Plan responsive crops and focal positions; verify the subject remains visible at narrow widths.
6. Implement optimized formats, dimensions, responsive sources, lazy loading where appropriate, and explicit aspect ratios/dimensions to limit layout shift.
7. Provide useful alternative text for informative images; use empty alt text for decorative images.

## Integrity and licensing
- Never fabricate product screenshots, customer logos, press coverage, testimonials, or evidence.
- Do not scrape or reuse images without permission.
- Verify license, attribution, commercial use, modification, and redistribution requirements.
- Avoid hotlinking third-party assets in production unless explicitly permitted and operationally appropriate.
- Do not create misleading synthetic imagery presented as documentary evidence.

## Quality checks
Review crop, sharpness, color treatment, visual hierarchy, responsive focal point, file size, loading behavior, alt text, and consistency across the site.

## Deliverable
Provide an asset plan with purpose, source/rights status, aspect ratio, crop/focal guidance, responsive behavior, optimization strategy, and alt-text intent.
