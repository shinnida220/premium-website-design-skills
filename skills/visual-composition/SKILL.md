# Visual Composition

Compose pages around a clear story and user task. Layout is not decoration; it directs attention and makes information understandable.

## Before layout

Identify the page's audience, intent, primary action, supporting actions, required content, trust evidence, and likely scan path. Use real content or realistic content-shaped examples; do not design around meaningless filler.

## Composition workflow

1. Establish a hierarchy: primary message, supporting explanation, proof/context, and action.
2. Choose a grid appropriate to the content. Define max-width, columns, gutters, and alignment relationships.
3. Sequence sections as a narrative: orient, explain value, substantiate, resolve objections, and provide next steps where relevant.
4. Create rhythm through deliberate variation in density, scale, alignment, and whitespace.
5. Give each section a clear purpose; remove sections that merely repeat the same claim.
6. Use asymmetry, overlap, large type, image crops, or negative space only when they improve the concept and remain usable.
7. Design transitions between sections, not just isolated blocks.
8. Check long headings, localization, variable content, and realistic asset proportions.

## Visual hierarchy

- One dominant focal point per major region.
- Clear heading levels and readable line lengths.
- Strong alignment and intentional spacing relationships.
- CTA prominence proportional to importance, without making every element compete.
- Images should add meaning, evidence, or atmosphere—not fill empty rectangles.
- Repeated cards should not become the default answer to every content problem.

## Responsive composition

Recompose at narrow widths: reorder content where needed, simplify grids, adjust type scale, preserve important image context, and maintain CTA visibility. Do not simply shrink desktop columns until they fit.

## Deliverable

Provide a page outline with section purpose, hierarchy, layout pattern, content/asset needs, and responsive changes. During critique, identify the largest composition defects and recommend concrete fixes.
