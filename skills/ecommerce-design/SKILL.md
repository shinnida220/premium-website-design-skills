# E-commerce Design

Build a shopping experience that helps people choose confidently and understand the full cost and consequences of purchase.

## Journey coverage
Review discovery, category/search, product detail, variant selection, cart, checkout, payment result, order confirmation, account/order tracking, delivery, returns, and support as relevant.

## Product discovery
- Make categories, search, filters, sorting, and product comparison understandable.
- Preserve selected filters and provide useful empty results.
- Use accurate product imagery, multiple angles, and honest availability.
- Avoid fake reviews, ratings, stock counts, and promotional claims.

## Product detail
- Show price, currency, variant, material/specification, dimensions, availability, delivery, returns, and relevant restrictions clearly.
- Make variant selection explicit and prevent invalid combinations.
- Provide accessible image galleries and meaningful zoom behavior.
- Put important purchase information near the add-to-cart action.

## Cart and checkout
- Show item, quantity, discounts, taxes, delivery, and total transparently.
- Minimize unnecessary fields and support guest checkout where appropriate.
- Explain errors and preserve entered data.
- Do not preselect optional add-ons or consent.
- Provide secure, clear payment handoff and a reliable confirmation state.
- Design for interruption and recovery.

## Mobile and accessibility
Ensure product imagery, variant controls, cart actions, and checkout remain usable on narrow screens. Support keyboard, screen readers, zoom, clear focus, and non-color state cues.

## Deliverable
Provide journey map, page/state inventory, product data requirements, interaction rules, responsive behavior, trust/clarity checks, and acceptance tests.
