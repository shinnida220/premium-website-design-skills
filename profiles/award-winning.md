# Award-winning / Experimental Profile

## Best suited to
Creative studios, portfolios, cultural projects, editorial experiences, campaigns, and brands where expressive visual storytelling is central.

## Design intent
Create a memorable, ownable experience with a clear conceptual point of view. Experimental does not mean confusing, inaccessible, or unusable.

## Direction
- Begin with a strong creative concept and build the composition around it.
- Treat typography, image sequencing, pacing, and whitespace as expressive tools.
- Use unexpected grids, scale shifts, asymmetry, and transitions when they reinforce the story.
- Give motion a narrative or functional purpose; provide reduced-motion alternatives.
- Make navigation discoverable even when it is unconventional.
- Preserve readable content, focus visibility, and usable touch targets.
- Test the experience without animation to ensure its structure still makes sense.

## Avoid
Novelty for its own sake, inaccessible cursor tricks, hidden essential navigation, motion that blocks reading, illegible type, and direct imitation of award-site references.

## Success criteria
The experience has a clear concept, strong recall, coherent pacing, and a usable path through its content on both desktop and mobile.
