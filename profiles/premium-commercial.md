# Premium / Commercial Profile

## Best suited to
SaaS, product marketing, professional services, premium consumer brands, and product launches where visitors need to understand value and take action.

## Design intent
Communicate confidence, clarity, quality, and momentum. Premium should come from disciplined hierarchy, typography, composition, content, and detail—not gratuitous effects.

## Direction
- Lead with a clear value proposition and a purposeful primary action.
- Use a restrained, intentional palette with a distinctive brand accent.
- Establish typographic contrast between display, heading, body, and utility text.
- Use high-quality product imagery, editorial photography, or meaningful diagrams.
- Prefer a few strong compositions over a long sequence of identical cards.
- Use surface effects, gradients, and motion selectively and consistently.
- Make pricing, proof, security, and objection-handling easy to find when relevant.

## Avoid
Generic SaaS hero templates, unsupported conversion claims, meaningless logo strips, excessive rounded cards, decorative gradients without a role, and CTA repetition that makes every section feel like an advertisement.

## Success criteria
Visitors can quickly identify what is offered, who it is for, why it matters, and what to do next. The site feels recognizably branded and works at mobile widths.
