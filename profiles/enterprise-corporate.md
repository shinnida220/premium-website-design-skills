# Enterprise / Corporate Profile

## Best suited to
B2B platforms, finance, healthcare, infrastructure, enterprise software, and products with complex information or high trust requirements.

## Design intent
Make complex information feel understandable and dependable. Professional does not have to mean bland.

## Direction
- Prioritize information architecture and predictable navigation.
- Explain the product in concrete language; distinguish facts from claims.
- Use typography and spacing to make dense information scannable.
- Use restrained color with clear semantic roles and visible states.
- Present proof, compliance, integrations, documentation, and support accurately.
- Ensure tables, forms, charts, and data states work on smaller screens.
- Use consistent components without making every page visually identical.

## Avoid
Unsubstantiated trust badges, fake customer logos, tiny low-contrast metadata, decorative complexity, and navigation that hides important product information.

## Success criteria
Users can understand capabilities, evaluate trust, find the information they need, and complete key tasks with predictable behavior.
