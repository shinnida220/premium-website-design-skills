# Hybrid Profile

## Best suited to
Modern B2B brands, developer tools, fintech, professional products, and services that need both credibility and a distinctive identity.

## Design intent
Combine a reliable usability foundation with selective expressive moments. The interface should feel branded without sacrificing clarity.

## Direction
- Keep navigation, forms, content structure, and core journeys familiar.
- Express brand character through typography, imagery, color, and a few signature compositions.
- Use stronger visual moments for the hero, product explanation, or editorial sections.
- Keep data-heavy and transactional areas restrained and legible.
- Use motion to clarify state or create a controlled transition.
- Maintain consistent design tokens across expressive and functional areas.

## Avoid
Two competing visual systems, decorative hero language that does not match the product UI, and a marketing site that promises a different experience from the product itself.

## Success criteria
The site feels distinctive at first glance and remains calm, predictable, and accessible when users need to understand information or complete a task.
