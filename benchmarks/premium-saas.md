# Benchmark 01 — Premium SaaS marketing site

## Scenario

Create a polished marketing website for **Northstar**, a fictional workflow planning SaaS for small operations teams. The product helps teams coordinate recurring work, assign ownership, and see delivery risks. Do not claim integrations, security certifications, customer adoption, or quantified outcomes that are not supplied here.

## Audience and goal

- Audience: operations leads and team managers at 10–200-person companies.
- Primary goal: get a qualified visitor to start a product trial.
- Secondary goal: understand the product workflow and pricing approach.
- Profile: Premium / Commercial with restrained product-led clarity.

## Required routes and sections

- Home: differentiated hero, product explanation, workflow, use cases, pricing teaser, FAQ, final CTA.
- Pricing: at least two clearly differentiated plans with transparent placeholder pricing labelled as illustrative.
- Product: explain plan → assign → monitor with a truthful, original UI illustration.

## Required interactions

- Navigation and mobile menu.
- Primary and secondary CTA destinations.
- Pricing toggle only if both values are clearly labelled and internally consistent.
- FAQ disclosure with keyboard support.
- Email capture form with validation, pending, success, and error states. No real submission endpoint is required; clearly communicate demo behavior.

## Content constraints

Use only the fictional product description above. Any illustrative interface data must be labelled as sample data. No fake testimonials, customer logos, ratings, usage numbers, or security badges.

## Required viewports

360×800, 390×844, 768×1024, 1366×768, 1440×900.

## Evaluation focus

Art direction and typography; hero clarity; believable product visualization; pricing comprehension; CTA hierarchy; mobile navigation; form usability; performance-conscious assets.

## Acceptance criteria

- Visitor can explain the product and intended audience from the first screen.
- Primary CTA is visually clear and has a working destination.
- Pricing language is transparent and not misleading.
- All interactions work with keyboard and touch.
- No horizontal overflow at required widths.
