# Benchmark 05 — Creative studio portfolio

## Scenario

Create a portfolio website for **Form & Field**, a fictional independent creative studio working across digital experiences, identity systems, and editorial design.

## Audience and goal

- Audience: founders, marketing leads, and cultural organizations seeking a creative partner.
- Primary goal: initiate a project conversation.
- Secondary goal: understand the studio's approach and selected work.
- Profile: Award-winning / Experimental, while preserving usability and content clarity.

## Required routes and sections

- Home: expressive hero, selected projects, studio point of view, capabilities, contact CTA.
- Work index: project cards with meaningful metadata and clear navigation.
- Project detail: challenge, approach, contribution, outcome. Use hypothetical framing where no real case evidence exists.
- Contact: concise inquiry form with validation and clear demo behavior.

## Required interactions

- Responsive navigation.
- Project card and detail navigation.
- Optional purposeful motion with reduced-motion alternative.
- Contact form with keyboard-operable validation and feedback.

## Content constraints

No fabricated client identities, awards, project metrics, testimonials, or published work claims. Projects may be explicitly marked as concept studies. Do not copy the visual identity of a reference studio.

## Required viewports

360×800, 768×1024, 1366×768, 1440×900.

## Evaluation focus

Distinctive concept; typography and composition; editorial rhythm; image direction; motion restraint; responsive integrity; accessibility; narrative clarity.

## Acceptance criteria

- The visual concept is recognizable and consistent across routes.
- Experimental layouts never compromise navigation or reading order.
- Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Work and project claims are clearly distinguished from concept material.
