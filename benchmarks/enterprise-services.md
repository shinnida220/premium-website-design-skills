# Benchmark 02 — Enterprise services website

## Scenario

Create a website for **Meridian Infrastructure Advisory**, a fictional consultancy that helps mid-market organizations plan cloud migrations, modernize legacy platforms, and improve operational reliability.

## Audience and goal

- Audience: CIOs, CTOs, procurement leads, and engineering directors.
- Primary goal: request a consultation.
- Secondary goal: understand service scope and delivery approach.
- Profile: Enterprise / Corporate.

## Required routes and sections

- Home: clear positioning, service overview, delivery approach, sectors served (do not invent named clients), consultation CTA.
- Services: cloud migration, platform modernization, reliability engineering.
- About: principles, team approach without fabricated biographies or credentials.
- Contact: consultation form and realistic contact placeholders explicitly marked for replacement.

## Required interactions

- Responsive navigation.
- Service detail links.
- Contact form with required-field validation, accessible error summary, pending, success, and failure states.
- Clear privacy note explaining demo form behavior.

## Content constraints

Do not invent client names, case-study results, certifications, partner badges, team credentials, or compliance claims. If example outcomes are needed, label them as hypothetical.

## Required viewports

360×800, 768×1024, 1366×768, 1920×1080.

## Evaluation focus

Trust without fabricated proof; information architecture; restrained but distinctive brand expression; readability; consultation journey; form accessibility; clear service differentiation.

## Acceptance criteria

- Services and target audience are immediately understandable.
- Claims are supportable by the supplied brief.
- Contact journey is operable and has useful feedback.
- Long-form content remains scannable on mobile and desktop.
