# Example project briefs

Use these examples to understand the level of context that helps an agent make better decisions. They are illustrative, not templates that must be copied.

## Premium B2B SaaS marketing site

- Product: workflow platform for operations teams.
- Audience: operations leaders and team managers at mid-sized businesses.
- Primary goal: book a qualified demo.
- Secondary goal: understand integrations and security posture.
- Pages: home, product, integrations, security, pricing, contact.
- Profile: Premium / Commercial.
- Constraints: existing Next.js app, Tailwind, approved brand mark, no verified customer logos yet.
- Success: clear positioning, credible product explanation, working demo request flow, responsive behavior, accessible forms.
- Important caveat: do not fabricate customer metrics or certifications.

## Experimental creative portfolio

- Product: independent motion and design studio.
- Audience: creative directors and potential collaborators.
- Primary goal: explore selected work and make contact.
- Pages: home, work index, project details, about, contact.
- Profile: Award-winning / Experimental.
- Constraints: project imagery supplied by studio; performance budget; reduced-motion support.
- Success: memorable visual identity, clear project storytelling, intuitive navigation, mobile experience that retains the concept.

## Enterprise fintech platform

- Product: business payments and reconciliation platform.
- Audience: finance teams, founders, and procurement leads.
- Primary goal: request a consultation or begin a verified onboarding process.
- Pages: home, platform, use cases, security, resources, contact.
- Profile: Hybrid or Enterprise / Corporate depending on positioning.
- Constraints: all claims require approval; high trust expectations; accessible forms; no invented compliance badges.
- Success: clear explanation of money movement, transparent trust information, strong information hierarchy, no misleading promises.

## Existing product redesign

- Product: established web application with active users.
- Audience: known from product analytics/research.
- Primary goal: improve task completion and reduce support friction.
- Scope: selected routes and components only.
- Profile: chosen after auditing current brand and user expectations.
- Constraints: preserve routes, data contracts, existing component conventions, and analytics.
- Success: no regression in core journeys; measured improvement where instrumentation exists; verified responsive and keyboard behavior.
